## FFmpeg
TailPad.exe bundles an unmodified **FFmpeg** executable (LGPL build by BtbN, https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds) for remote-play video capture and encoding.
FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later. Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds.
TailPad runs it as a separate program; you can replace it by placing your own ffmpeg.exe next to TailPad.exe.

Also used: Nefarius.ViGEm.Client (MIT), QRCoder (MIT), NAudio (MIT), Concentus (BSD-3-Clause).
